workbuddy

A private, single-user client for the owner's own Google account.

workbuddy is not a public product. It is a personal automation tool that runs locally on its owner's computer. There is no sign-up, no accounts, and no other users. Only the project owner can authorize it against his own Google account.

What it does

workbuddy automates the owner's own day-to-day administrative work by calling Google APIs on his behalf, using OAuth tokens that he grants himself. Specifically it can:

  • List, read and organise files in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Read and update spreadsheets in the owner's Google Sheets.
  • Read, create and update events in the owner's Google Calendar.

All processing happens on the owner's own machine. workbuddy has no backend service and no database of Google user data.

Who can use it

Nobody but the owner. The OAuth client is not published for general use and access is limited to the owner's own Google account.
